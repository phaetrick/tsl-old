Features
  • Live Recording: Records everything currently outputting from the speakers before it reaches the DAC. Output format options include stereo 16-bit WAV, MP3, or FLAC (16 or 24-bit), with the sample rate matching the default sample rate of the device (usually 44.1 or 48 kHz).

  • Saving and Restoring MIDI Mappings.
  • LFO Syncing with external MIDI Clock.
  • Mono- and Stereo Effects: Includes a variety of effects such as reverbs, delays, EQs, and phasers. The manual provides descriptions of some of the effects included in the Upgrade.
